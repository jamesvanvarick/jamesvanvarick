AAON, Inc.

NASDAQ: AAON · HVAC / Building Products

Original thesis. I initiated AAON at $88.29 in August 2025 with a $107 price target. The thesis centered on BASX's exposure to rapidly growing data-center cooling demand, with product differentiation and decarbonization supporting the core AAON brand.

Update. By the June 2026 update, shares had risen 44% to $127.19. BASX backlog reached $1.6bn, the core AAON brand returned to growth, and several operational disruptions were beginning to ease. I raised my price target to $166.

Global Ship Lease, Inc.

NYSE: GSL · Containership Leasing

Original thesis. I initiated GSL at $23.18 in March 2025. The company combined contracted cash flows, rapid deleveraging, and low operating breakevens while trading at roughly 2.4x earnings. It led me to a $68 price target.

Update. By the July 2026 update, shares had risen 78% to $41.31. In this report I moved to evaluating GSL through a credit lens rather than setting a price target: with net leverage down to 0.4x EBITDA, I believe the company can service and retire its obligations, including the dividend, through 2030 even if charter rates fall significantly.

Hyliion Holdings Corp.

NYSE American: HYLN · Industrial Manufacturing / Electrical Equipment

Primer. Hyliion develops KARNO, a modular, fuel-agnostic on-site power generator being pitched to data centers, defense, and commercial facilities. The company preserved substantial capital after abandoning its original electrified-truck-powertrain business, and KARNO has Navy validation and real technical differentiation. But its largest markets remain pre-commercial: the $400m+ pipeline is entirely non-binding LOIs, roughly a third of it tied to a counterparty a short report flagged as a January 2026 incorporation, and manufacturing capacity is still undisclosed.

This is too early-stage for a financial model or a price target, so I'm not issuing one. I'm publishing it as a primer and will keep watching for a handful of concrete milestones: full 200 kW power output, UL certification, a converted LOI, and first commercial revenue with disclosed gross margins.
